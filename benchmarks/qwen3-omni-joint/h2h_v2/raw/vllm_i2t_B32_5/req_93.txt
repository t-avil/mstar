**What is Kibbeh?**

Kibbeh is a traditional dish from the Middle East, particularly popular in Lebanon, Syria, and Iraq. It is typically made from a mixture of ground meat (usually lamb or beef) and bulgur wheat, seasoned with spices like allspice, cinnamon, and cumin.

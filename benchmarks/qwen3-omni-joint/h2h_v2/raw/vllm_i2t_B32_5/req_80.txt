Here are the key features that lead to this identification:

*   **Pleated Top:** The most distinctive feature is the intricate, pleated top of the dumpling. This is created by pinching the edges of the dough together, a hallmark of this specific dumpling style.
*   **Steamed Preparation:** The dumplings are arranged on a round, perforated metal tray, which is a traditional bamboo steamer basket. This indicates they are cooked by steaming.
*   **Dough and Shape:** The dough appears thin and delicate, which is necessary to contain the hot soup filling without breaking during steaming. The overall shape is a small, round pouch with a gathered top.

Xiaolongbao are a famous type of soup dumpling originating from the Jiangnan region of China, particularly associated with the city of Wuxi. They are known for their thin, translucent skin and a rich, savory broth and filling inside, typically made with pork.

Based on the visual characteristics of the food in the image, this dish is most likely **Xiaolongbao** (also known as **soup dumplings**).

Here is a breakdown of the identifying features:

*   **Steamed Dumpling:** The dumplings are cooked by steaming, as indicated by the bamboo steamer basket they are served in.
*   **Pleated Top:** The most distinctive feature is the top of the dumpling, which is gathered and pleated into a small, elegant bun shape. This is a hallmark of Xiaolongbao.
*   **Thin Skin:** The dumpling wrappers appear thin and translucent, which is necessary to contain the hot soup inside without breaking during cooking.
*   **Soup Filling:** The name "soup dumpling" comes from the fact that they are filled with a savory broth (usually pork-based) and gelatin. When steamed, the gelatin melts into a hot, flavorful soup. The small dark spots on the paper liner are likely droplets of this soup that have leaked from the dumplings during steaming.

In summary, the combination of being a steamed dumpling with a pleated top and a soup filling is the defining characteristic of Xiaolongbao, a famous dish

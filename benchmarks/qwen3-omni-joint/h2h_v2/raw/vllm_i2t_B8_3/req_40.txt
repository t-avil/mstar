Based on the visual elements in the image, the dish shown is **Thai Fried Rice**, also known as **Khao Pad**.

Here are the key characteristics that lead to this identification:

*   **Color:** The rice has a distinct yellowish hue, which is characteristic of Thai fried rice. This color comes from turmeric and/or egg, which are mixed into the rice during cooking.
*   **Garnishes:** The dish is garnished with fresh, raw vegetables, which is typical for this preparation. You can clearly see:
    *   Slices of cucumber
    *   A slice of tomato
    *   A wedge of lime (served on the side for squeezing over the rice)
    *   A few stalks of green onion (scallions)
*   **Texture:** The individual grains of rice are visible and appear to be separate, which is the desired texture for fried rice.

This combination of a molded, yellow-colored rice dish served with fresh garnishes is a classic presentation of Thai Fried Rice.

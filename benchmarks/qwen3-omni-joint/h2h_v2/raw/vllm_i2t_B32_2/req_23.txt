Based on the visual characteristics of the food in the image, this dish is most likely **Xiaolongbao** (also known as **soup dumplings**).

Here is a breakdown of the identifying features:

*   **Steamed Dumpling:** The dumplings are cooked by steaming, as evidenced by the bamboo steamer basket they are served in.
*   **Pleated Top:** The most distinctive feature is the top of the dumpling, which is gathered and pleated into a small, elegant bun-like shape. This is a hallmark of Xiaolongbao.
*   **Thin Skin:** The dumpling wrappers appear very thin and translucent, which is necessary to contain the hot soup inside without breaking during cooking.
*   **Soup Filling:** The name "soup dumpling" comes from the fact that they are filled with a savory broth (usually made from pork stock and gelatin) that melts into liquid during the steaming process. The liquid on the parchment paper in the steamer is likely the soup that has seeped out from the dumplings.

In summary, the combination of being a steamed dumpling with a pleated top and a thin wrapper designed to hold a soup filling is the classic definition of Xiaolongbao.

Based on the visual characteristics of the food in the image, this dish is **Xiaolongbao** (also known as **soup dumplings**).

Here are the key features that lead to this identification:

*   **Pleated Top:** The most distinctive feature is the top of the dumpling, which is gathered and pleated into a tight, decorative swirl. This is a hallmark of the xiaolongbao-making technique.
*   **Steamed Preparation:** The dumplings are arranged on a round, perforated metal tray, which is a traditional bamboo steamer basket. This indicates they are cooked by steaming.
*   **Dough and Shape:** The dough appears thin and delicate, which is necessary to contain the hot soup filling without breaking during steaming. The overall shape is a small, plump, dome-like pouch.

Xiaolongbao are a famous type of soup dumpling originating from the Jiangnan region of China, particularly associated with the city of Wuxi. They are filled with a mixture of minced pork and a gelatinized pork stock that melts into a savory broth when steamed.

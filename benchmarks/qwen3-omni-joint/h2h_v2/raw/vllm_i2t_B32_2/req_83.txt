Based on the image provided, this dish is most commonly known as **edamame**.

Here is a more detailed description of the food:

*   **Main Ingredient**: The green pods are **edamame**, which are immature soybeans. They are typically boiled or steamed and served as a healthy snack or appetizer.
*   **Toppings**: The dish is garnished with a **slice of lemon** and a sprinkle of **coarse salt**. The lemon adds a bright, acidic flavor that complements the mild, slightly sweet taste of the soybeans, while the salt enhances the overall flavor.
*   **Serving Style**: It is served warm in the pod, which is the traditional way to eat edamame. The diner squeezes the beans out of the pod with their teeth and discards the pod.

This preparation is a very popular appetizer in Japanese cuisine and is also widely enjoyed in many other parts of the world.

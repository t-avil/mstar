 on the visual characteristics of the food in the image, this dish isBased **Xiaolongbao** (also known as **soup dumplings**).

Here are the key features that identify it:

*   **Steamed Dumplings:** They are small, round dumplings with a thin, translucent wrapper, which is characteristic of dumplings steamed in a bamboo basket (a *zheng*).
*   **Pleated Top:** The dumplings have a distinct pleated top, which is a signature of the *xiaolongbao* style.
*   **Filling:** The name "soup dumpling" comes from the fact that they contain a hot, savory broth inside, along with a filling, typically made of minced pork. The broth is created by including a gelatinous meat jelly in the filling, which melts into soup when steamed.

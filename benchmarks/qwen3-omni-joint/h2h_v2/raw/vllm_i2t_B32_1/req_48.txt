Based on the visual characteristics of the dish, this food is a type of **taco**.

Here is a breakdown of the components that lead to this identification:

*   **Tortilla:** The base is a soft, round flatbread that has been grilled or seared, evident from the distinct char marks. This is a common preparation for tacos.
*   **Protein:** The filling consists of grilled, flaked fish, which appears to be a white fish like tilapia or cod.
*   **Toppings:** The taco is generously topped with fresh ingredients, including:
    *   Arugula (rocket)
    *   Thinly sliced red onions
    *   A lemon wedge on the side for squeezing
    *   A small side of what looks like a slaw made of shredded red cabbage and white onion.

This specific combination of grilled fish, arugula, and red onion on a grilled tortilla is a very popular style of taco, often referred to as a **Fish Taco**.

## 1. Grid part A — what vLLM-Omni actually changed, 0.21 → 0.22

Columns: (1) technique, one sentence; (2) what's actually going on;
(3) did we try it / is it applicable; (4) our measured results; (5) why the
results came out that way.

| # | Technique (one-sentence) | What's actually going on (detail) | Tried / applicable in M*? | Our results | Deep theoretical justification |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Full-engine rebase to vLLM v0.22** — the omni layer moved onto the modern V1 engine, activating everything below. | `f92d84fc` (#3891), 62 files. The carrier commit: the omni thinker now runs under core-vLLM V1's scheduler, model runner, and compilation stack instead of the 0.21-era path. On 0.21, sustained mixed prefill+decode demoted decode steps to PIECEWISE/eager attention (their compilation.py:604-606 era); on 0.22 uniform decode steps stay FULL-cudagraph. | Not "appliable" as such — it's their platform shift. | A2 | **Async scheduling / overlapped sampled-token D2H** — sampled ids copy to host on a side stream; the host only blocks when it actually needs them. | In `f92d84fc`: `vllm_omni/worker/gpu_model_runner.py:249-266` — D2H of sampled tokens is issued `non_blocking` on a copy stream with `async_copy_ready_event`; readback+detokenize overlap the next forward instead of stalling it. | **Yes — this is V1, the headliner on our option board, not yet implemented.** Precursors tried: E9 direct GPU token feed (wash), E10 two-step decode (flat), W3 run-ahead (skipped after design audit). | E9: 0.96–1.07× (wash, mechanism validated). E10: 0.97–1.00× (flat). Both **pre-dated every main-thread win** and are queued for re-test (R2). | Our GPU-thread submit path was already host-sync-free via speculation + double-buffered plans, so removing the token-feed hop alone was worth ~0 (that's why E9/E10 were flat). | A3 | **Batch-level routed-experts bookkeeping** — one per-batch experts structure with async D2H instead of a per-request dict. | Also in `f92d84fc`: one `RoutedExpertsLists` per batch, `non_blocking=True` D2H, per-request slicing pushed to the scheduler (`gpu_model_runner.py:71-115`, `core/sched/utils.py`). | **Yes — same class as our biggest wins.** We did the per-rid→per-batch conversion on every hot loop we found. | E3 batched prepare/check_stop/send: i2t 1.13/1.22/1.15× (ab_stack). This *repaired their own regression*; it did not beat anyone's kernels. | N/A as a fix (their bug). Our kernel-level answer on the same block: **block-fp8 w8a8 MoE** (E1). Also fused router topk (E2). | E1: in-graph 1.44–1.61× vs bf16 at decode M; e2e i2t +13% at B8/B32, s2t +11%/+5%; text outputs identical; −27 GB VRAM. E2: 27.6→11.3 µs/layer. Their fp16-path fix just restored parity-class kernels — which is why "kernels are the same class" is true and the fight is host-side. |

| A6 | **Deepstack-under-compile fix** — vision inputs no longer break torch.compile, so the thinker decoder stays compiled + FULL-graph. | `bb9f21d0` (#3885): resize-on-demand persistent buffer for deepstack input embeds (`qwen3_omni_moe_thinker.py:1181-1188,1228`) removes the dynamic-shape re-trace that silently dropped the model to eager. Every % of the remaining gap is host-side Python, which is why the forward plan (V1 async sched, SCHED_PACK, staging/transport packs) is 100% host-side. |

## 4. Next steps (as scoped — replaces "image TTFT + prefix caching")

1. **Canonical-pair sweep** of ab75034 + final flags (both prior attempts aborted; GPUs now free) — turns 0.83×-measured/0.90×-projected into one number.
2. **V1 async scheduling** (their A2, our design adapted to the two-thread worker) — the last structural mechanism they have and we don't; est. +5–10%.
3. **Mid-batch B2–B4 diagnosis** (only unexplained region of the curve).
4. Host-floor packs (scheduler/transport/staging) + the 24-option board in PLAN_BEAT_VLLM_V4.md, each dynflags-flippable for ~5-min A/B signal.
5. Image TTFT remains real future work (0.43 vs 0.16 s at B32) — chunked-prefill piggybacking is the mechanism, our W5/V2 machinery is the vehicle. Prefix caching: not applicable to these benchmarks; only relevant if production traffic has shared prefixes.

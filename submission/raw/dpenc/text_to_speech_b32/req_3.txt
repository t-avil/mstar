The French Revolution (1789–1799) was a period of radical social and political upheaval in France that overthrew the monarchy, ended the ancien régime, and led to the rise of democratic ideals. Sparked by widespread discontent with inequality, financial crisis, and Enlightenment ideas, it resulted in the execution of King Louis XVI and the Reign of Terror under Robespierre. Ultimately, it paved the way for modern France, influencing democratic movements worldwide.<|im_end|>

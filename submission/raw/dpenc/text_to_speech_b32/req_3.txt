The French Revolution (1789–1799) was a period of radical social and political upheaval in France that led to the end of the monarchy and the rise of democratic ideals. Sparked by widespread discontent with the monarchy, economic hardship, and Enlightenment ideas, it resulted in the overthrow of King Louis XVI and the establishment of a republic. The revolution was marked by key events such as the Storming of the Bastille, the Reign of Terror, and the eventual rise of Napoleon Bonaparte.<|im_end|>

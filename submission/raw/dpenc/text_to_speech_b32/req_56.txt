The terms **CV** (Curriculum Vitae) and **resume** are often used interchangeably, but they have distinct differences in length, content, and usage depending on the region and context. Here's a breakdown:

### 1. **Length**
- **CV**: Typically **longer**, often **2+ pages** (can be longer depending on experience).
- **Resume**: Usually **shorter**, ideally **1 page** for early-career professionals, up to **2 pages** for experienced candidates.

### 2. **Purpose**
- **CV**: A **comprehensive document** that details your **entire academic and professional history**. It's used to showcase your **scholarly achievements** and **research experience**.
- **Resume**: A **concise summary** of your **most relevant skills and experiences** tailored to a specific job.

### 3. **Content**
- **CV** includes:
  - Full work history
  - Education (including thesis/dissertation topics)
  - Publications
  - Research projects
  - Grants and fellowships
  - Teaching experience
  - Conferences and presentations
  - Awards and honors
  - Professional memberships
  - Languages and other relevant skills

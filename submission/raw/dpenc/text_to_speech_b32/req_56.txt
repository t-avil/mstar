The terms **CV** (Curriculum Vitae) and **resume** are often used interchangeably, but they have distinct differences in length, content, and usage depending on the region and context. Here's a breakdown:

### 1. **Length**
- **CV**: Typically **longer**, often **2+ pages** (can be longer depending on experience).
- **Resume**: Usually **shorter**, ideally **1 page** (2 pages max for experienced professionals).

### 2. **Purpose & Usage**
- **CV**: Commonly used in **academic, research, medical, or international contexts** (especially in Europe, Asia, Africa, and Australia). It's standard for job applications in academia, teaching, or research roles.
- **Resume**: Preferred in **industry, business, and corporate environments**, especially in the **United States and Canada**.

### 3. **Content**
- **CV**:
  - Includes **education**, **research**, **publications**, **presentations**, **teaching experience**, **awards**, **grants**, **conferences**, and **professional affiliations**.
  - More **comprehensive** and **detailed**.
  - Often includes **personal information** like date of birth, nationality

The main difference between **Roman numerals** and **Arabic numerals** lies in their **origin, structure, and usage**:

### 1. **Origin**
- **Roman numerals**: Developed by the ancient Romans around 500 BCE. They are based on letters from the Latin alphabet.
- **Arabic numerals**: Originated in India around the 5th century CE and were later adopted and spread by Arab mathematicians. They are now the standard numeral system used worldwide.

### 2. **Symbols**
- **Roman numerals** use letters to represent numbers:
  - I = 1, V = 5, X = 10, L = 50, C = 100, D = 500, M = 1000
  - Example: **XIV** = 14 (10 + 4)
- **Arabic numerals** use digits 0–9:
  - Example: **14** = 14

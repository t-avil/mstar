The capital of France is **Paris**.

Paris is one of the most iconic and culturally rich cities in the world, renowned for its art, fashion, gastronomy, and history. Located on the River Seine in northern France, it is the country's political, economic, and cultural center. With a population of over 2 million people in the city proper and more than 12 million in the metropolitan area, Paris is the largest city in France and one of the most visited cities globally.

**Key Features of Paris:**

1. **Historical Significance**: Paris has been a major European city for centuries. It played a central role in the French Revolution and has been the seat of French government since the Middle Ages. The city is home to numerous historical landmarks, including the Notre-Dame Cathedral, the Louvre Museum, and the Palace of Versailles (just outside the city).

2. **Cultural Hub**: Paris is a global center for art and fashion. It hosts world-famous museums like the Louvre (home to the Mona Lisa), Musée d'Orsay, and Centre Pompidou. The city is also a fashion capital, with major fashion houses like Chanel, Dior, and Yves Saint Laurent headquartered there.

*Romeo and Juliet* is a tragic play written by William Shakespeare, believed to have been composed around 1595–1596. It tells the story of two young lovers from feuding families in Verona, Italy, whose passionate romance ends in tragedy.

**Plot Summary:**

1. **The Feud:** The story begins with a long-standing and bitter feud between two noble families in Verona: the Montagues and the Capulets. The conflict is so deep that even servants from both families engage in brawls.

2. **Romeo's Unrequited Love:** Romeo Montague is in love with a woman named Rosaline, but she does not return his affections. His friends, Benvolio and Mercutio, encourage him to attend a Capulet party where he might meet someone new.

3. **Meeting Juliet:** At the Capulet ball, Romeo sees Juliet Capulet for the first time and is instantly smitten. Despite knowing she is a Capulet, he falls deeply in love with her. Juliet, too, is drawn to him. They share a famous balcony scene where they declare their love and vow to marry.

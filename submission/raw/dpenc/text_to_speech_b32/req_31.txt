*Romeo and Juliet* is a tragic play written by William Shakespeare, believed to have been composed around 1595–1596. It tells the story of two young lovers from feuding families in Verona, Italy, whose passionate romance ends in tragedy.

**Plot Summary:**

1. **The Feud:** The story begins with a long-standing and bitter feud between two noble families in Verona—the Montagues and the Capulets. The conflict is so deep that even servants from both families engage in brawls.

2. **Romeo's Unrequited Love:** Romeo Montague is initially in love with a woman named Rosaline, but she does not return his affections. At a Capulet party, Romeo sneaks in and sees Juliet Capulet, the daughter of the enemy family. Instantly, they fall deeply in love at first sight.

3. **Secret Romance:** Despite their families' hatred, Romeo and Juliet secretly marry the next day with the help of Friar Laurence, a kind and wise priest who hopes their union might end the feud.

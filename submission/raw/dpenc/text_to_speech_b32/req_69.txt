Noise-canceling headphones work by using a technology called **active noise control (ANC)** to reduce unwanted ambient sounds. Here's how it works:

1. **Microphones**: The headphones have built-in microphones that pick up external sounds, such as engine noise, airplane hum, or office chatter.

2. **Signal Processing**: The microphones send the sound signals to a small processor inside the headphones. This processor analyzes the incoming noise in real time.

3. **Generate Anti-Noise**: The processor then creates a new sound wave that is the **exact opposite (inverted phase)** of the detected noise. This is based on the principle of **destructive interference**—when two sound waves of the same frequency but opposite phase meet, they cancel each other out.

4. **Play Anti-Noise**: The headphones play this "anti-noise" sound through the speakers, which combines with the ambient noise and cancels it out.

5. **Result**: The user hears a quieter environment because the unwanted sounds are reduced or eliminated.

**Important Notes**:
- ANC is most effective for **low-frequency, continuous sounds** like airplane engines or air conditioning. It's less effective for sudden or high-frequency noises like voices or clattering.

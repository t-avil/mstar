*1984* is a dystopian novel by George Orwell, published in 1949. The story is set in a totalitarian society ruled by the Party, which exercises total control over every aspect of life in the fictional superstate of Oceania. The protagonist, Winston Smith, is a low-ranking member of the Party who works at the Ministry of Truth, where he alters historical records to align with the Party’s ever-changing narratives.

Winston harbors secret rebellious thoughts and begins to question the Party’s authority. He starts a forbidden romantic relationship with Julia, a fellow Party member who also resents the regime. Together, they seek freedom and truth, meeting with a mysterious figure named O'Brien, who claims to be part of the underground resistance known as the Brotherhood.

However, their rebellion is short-lived. They are betrayed and captured by the Thought Police. Winston is subjected to intense psychological and physical torture in the Ministry of Love, where he is forced to renounce his beliefs and betray Julia. Ultimately, he is broken and forced to love Big Brother, the Party’s omnipresent leader.

A **simile** and a **metaphor** are both figures of speech used to make comparisons, but they do so in different ways:

### **Simile**:
- A simile **explicitly compares** two things using words like **"like"** or **"as."**
- It makes the comparison **direct and clear**.
- Example: *"Her smile is as bright as the sun."*  
  → Here, the smile is compared to the sun using "as."

### **Metaphor**:
- A metaphor **directly states** that one thing **is** another, without using "like" or "as."
- It creates a **stronger, more imaginative connection** between the two things.
- Example: *"Her smile is the sun."*  
  → This suggests her smile **is** the sun, not just similar to it.

### Key Difference:
- **Simile**: Uses "like" or "as" to compare.
- **Metaphor**: Makes a direct comparison without those words.

Both are powerful tools in writing and poetry to create vivid imagery and emotional impact.<|im_end|>

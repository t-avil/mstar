The French Revolution (1789–1799) was a period of radical social and political upheaval in France that overthrew the monarchy, ended the ancien régime, and led to the rise of democratic ideals. Sparked by widespread discontent with economic hardship, social inequality, and Enlightenment ideas, it culminated in events like the Storming of the Bastille, the Reign of Terror, and the eventual rise of Napoleon Bonaparte. The revolution fundamentally transformed French society and inspired democratic movements across Europe and the world.<|im_end|>

Dehydration occurs when your body loses more fluids than it takes in, leading to an imbalance in essential electrolytes and fluids. The symptoms of dehydration can vary depending on its severity, ranging from mild to severe. Here are the common symptoms:

### Mild to Moderate Dehydration:
- **Thirst** – One of the earliest and most common signs.
- **Dry mouth and dry skin** – Reduced saliva production and decreased skin elasticity.
- **Decreased urine output** – Urine may be dark yellow or amber in color.
- **Fatigue or weakness** – Low fluid levels can reduce energy and cause tiredness.
- **Headache** – Often due to reduced blood flow and oxygen to the brain.
- **Dizziness or lightheadedness** – Especially when standing up quickly.
- **Dry eyes** – Reduced tear production.
- **Muscle cramps** – Due to electrolyte imbalances, especially sodium and potassium.

### Severe Dehydration:
- **Very dry skin and mucous membranes** – Skin may not bounce back when pinched (poor skin turgor).
- **Sunken eyes** – A visible sign of fluid loss.
- **Rapid heartbeat and breathing** – The body tries to compensate for low blood volume.

The capital of France is **Paris**.

Paris is one of the most iconic and culturally rich cities in the world, renowned for its art, fashion, gastronomy, and historical landmarks. Located on the River Seine in northern France, it is the country's political, economic, and cultural center.

Some key features of Paris include:

1. **Historical Significance**: Paris has been a major European city for centuries. It played a central role in the French Revolution and has been a hub of intellectual and artistic movements, including the Enlightenment, Romanticism, and the 20th-century avant-garde.

2. **Landmarks and Architecture**:
   - **Eiffel Tower**: Perhaps the most recognizable symbol of Paris, this iron lattice tower was built for the 1889 World's Fair and remains a global icon.
   - **Notre-Dame Cathedral**: A masterpiece of French Gothic architecture, located on the Île de la Cité. It suffered a devastating fire in 2019 but is undergoing restoration.
   - **Louvre Museum**: The world’s largest art museum and home to masterpieces like the *Mona Lisa* and the *Venus de Milo*.

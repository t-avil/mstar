The capital of France is **Paris**.

Paris is one of the most iconic and culturally rich cities in the world, renowned for its art, fashion, gastronomy, and historical landmarks. Located on the River Seine in northern France, it is the country's political, economic, and cultural center. With a population of over 2 million people in the city proper and more than 12 million in the metropolitan area, Paris is the largest city in France and one of the most visited cities globally.

**Key Features of Paris:**

1. **Historical and Architectural Landmarks:**
   - **Eiffel Tower**: Perhaps the most recognizable symbol of Paris, this iron lattice tower was built for the 1889 World's Fair and remains a major tourist attraction.
   - **Notre-Dame Cathedral**: A masterpiece of French Gothic architecture, located on the Île de la Cité. Though damaged by a fire in 2019, restoration efforts are ongoing.
   - **Louvre Museum**: The world's largest art museum and a historic monument, home to masterpieces like the *Mona Lisa* and the *Venus de Milo*.

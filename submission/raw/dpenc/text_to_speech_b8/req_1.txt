Certainly! Here's a clear explanation of the difference between **machine learning** and **deep learning**:

### 1. **Definition**
- **Machine Learning (ML):** A broad field of artificial intelligence where computers learn from data and improve their performance on a task over time without being explicitly programmed.
- **Deep Learning (DL):** A subset of machine learning that uses **artificial neural networks** with multiple layers (hence "deep") to model complex patterns in data.

---

### 2. **How They Work**
- **Machine Learning:**
  - Uses algorithms like decision trees, support vector machines (SVM), random forests, or linear regression.
  - Requires **feature engineering**: humans must manually select and extract relevant features from raw data.
  - Works well with structured data (e.g., spreadsheets, databases).

- **Deep Learning:**
  - Uses **neural networks** with many layers (e.g., convolutional neural networks for images, recurrent neural networks for sequences).
  - Automatically learns features from raw data — no need for manual feature extraction.
  - Excels with unstructured data like images, audio, and text.

---

### 3. **Data Requirements**
- **Machine Learning:** Can work well with smaller datasets

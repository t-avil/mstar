*Romeo and Juliet* is a tragic play written by William Shakespeare, believed to have been composed around 1595–1596. It tells the story of two young lovers from feuding families in Verona, Italy, whose passionate romance ends in tragedy.

**Plot Summary:**

1. **The Feud:** The story begins with a long-standing and bitter feud between two noble families in Verona—the Montagues and the Capulets. The conflict is so deep that even servants from both families engage in brawls.

2. **Romeo's Unrequited Love:** Romeo Montague is heartbroken over his unrequited love for a woman named Rosaline. He attends a Capulet party, where he sees Juliet Capulet for the first time and is instantly smitten.

3. **Love at First Sight:** Romeo and Juliet meet at the party and fall deeply in love at first sight. They learn of each other's identities too late—their families are enemies. Despite this, they quickly decide to marry.

4. **Secret Marriage:** With the help of Friar Laurence, a kind and wise priest, Romeo and Juliet are secretly married the next day. Friar Laurence hopes their union might end the feud

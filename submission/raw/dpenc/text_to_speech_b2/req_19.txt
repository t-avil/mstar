Dehydration occurs when your body loses more fluid than it takes in, leading to an imbalance in essential bodily functions. The symptoms of dehydration can vary depending on its severity, ranging from mild to severe. Here are the common symptoms:

**Mild to Moderate Dehydration:**
- Thirst
- Dry mouth and dry skin
- Decreased urine output or dark yellow urine
- Fatigue or weakness
- Headache
- Dizziness or lightheadedness
- Muscle cramps
- Dry eyes or reduced tear production
- Constipation

**Severe Dehydration:**
- Very dry skin and mucous membranes
- Rapid heartbeat and low blood pressure
- Rapid, shallow breathing
- Sunken eyes
- Low urine output or no urine for several hours
- Confusion or irritability
- Fainting or loss of consciousness
- In infants: sunken soft spot (fontanelle), no tears when crying, dry mouth, and fewer wet diapers

- **Infants and young children:** May show signs like dry mouth, crying without tears, sunken eyes, and fewer wet diapers.

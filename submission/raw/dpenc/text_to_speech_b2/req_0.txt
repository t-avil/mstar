The capital of France is **Paris**.

Paris is one of the most iconic and influential cities in the world, renowned for its rich history, art, fashion, cuisine, and culture. Located on the River Seine in northern France, it is the country's political, economic, and cultural center. With a population of over 2 million people in the city proper and more than 12 million in the metropolitan area, Paris is the largest city in France and one of the most visited cities globally.

**Key Features of Paris:**

1. **Historical Significance**: Paris has been a major European city for centuries. It was the capital of the Kingdom of France and later the French Republic. The city has witnessed pivotal historical events, including the French Revolution and both World Wars.

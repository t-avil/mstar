The French Revolution (1789–1799) was a period of radical social and political change in France, sparked by widespread discontent with the monarchy, economic hardship, and Enlightenment ideals. It led to the end of absolute monarchy, the rise of democratic principles, and the eventual establishment of a republic. The revolution was marked by key events such as the Storming of the Bastille, the Reign of Terror, and the rise and fall of figures like Robespierre and Napoleon Bonaparte.<|im_end|>

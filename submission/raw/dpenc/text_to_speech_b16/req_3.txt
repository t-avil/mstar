The French Revolution (1789–1799) was a period of radical social and political upheaval in France that overthrew the monarchy, ended feudal privileges, and led to the rise of democratic ideals. Sparked by widespread discontent with economic hardship, inequality, and Enlightenment ideas, it culminated in events like the Storming of the Bastille, the Reign of Terror, and the eventual rise of Napoleon Bonaparte. The revolution fundamentally transformed France’s political structure and inspired democratic movements worldwide.<|im_end|>

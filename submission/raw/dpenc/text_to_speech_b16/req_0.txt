The capital of France is **Paris**.

Paris is one of the most iconic and culturally rich cities in the world, often referred to as the "City of Light" (*La Ville Lumière*). Located on the River Seine in northern France, it is not only the political and economic heart of the country but also a global center for art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture.

**Key Features of Paris:**

1. **Historical Significance**: Paris has a history spanning over 2,000 years. It was originally a Gallo-Roman settlement known as Lutetia. Over the centuries, it evolved into a major European capital, playing a central role in events such as the French Revolution and both World Wars.

2. **Architecture and Landmarks**: The city is renowned for its stunning architecture, blending medieval, Renaissance, and modern styles. Some of its most famous landmarks include:
   - **Eiffel Tower**: An iron lattice tower built for the 1889 World's Fair, now a global symbol of Paris and France.

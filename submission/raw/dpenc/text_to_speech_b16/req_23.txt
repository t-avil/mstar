Great question! "Affect" and "effect" are often confused because they sound similar and both can be used as nouns and verbs, but they have different meanings and uses.

Here’s a breakdown:

### 1. **Affect (usually a verb)**
- **Meaning**: To influence or produce a change in something.
- **Example**:  
  - *The weather can affect your mood.*  
  - *The new policy will affect how we work.*

👉 Tip: Think of **"Affect" as Action** — it's something you *do* to something else.

---

### 2. **Effect (usually a noun)**
- **Meaning**: The result or outcome of a change.
- **Example**:  
  - *The effect of the new policy was positive.*  
  - *The medicine had a calming effect.*

👉 Tip: Think of **"Effect" as Result** — it's what happens *after* something.

---

### Quick Memory Trick:
- **A**ffect → **A**ction (verb)
- **E**ffect → **E**nd result (noun)

---

### Exceptions:
- **Affect** can also be a **noun** in psychology, meaning an emotional state or expression.

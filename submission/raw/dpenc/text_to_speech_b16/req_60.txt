The main difference between **Roman numerals** and **Arabic numerals** lies in their **origin, structure, and usage**:

### 1. **Origin and History**
- **Roman numerals**: Developed by the ancient Romans around 500 BCE. They were used throughout the Roman Empire and remained in use in Europe for centuries.
- **Arabic numerals**: Originated in India around the 5th century CE and were later adopted and spread by Arab mathematicians. They became the standard numeral system in Europe by the 12th century.

### 2. **Symbols and Representation**
- **Roman numerals** use **letters** from the Latin alphabet:
  - I = 1
  - V = 5
  - X = 10
  - L = 50
  - C = 100
  - D = 500
  - M = 1000
  - Numbers are formed by combining these letters (e.g., IV = 4, IX = 9, XVI = 16).

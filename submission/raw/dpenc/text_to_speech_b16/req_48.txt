The primary colors of light are **red, green, and blue**. These are known as the **additive primary colors** because when combined in different intensities, they can produce a wide range of other colors. This principle is used in devices like computer monitors, televisions, and smartphones, which use red, green, and blue (RGB) subpixels to create full-color images. When red, green, and blue light are combined at full intensity, they produce white light.<|im_end|>

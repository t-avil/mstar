The terms **CV** (Curriculum Vitae) and **resume** are often used interchangeably, but they have distinct differences in length, content, and usage depending on the region and context. Here's a breakdown of the key differences:

### 1. **Length**
- **CV**: Typically **longer**, often **2–5 pages or more**, depending on experience and academic achievements.
- **Resume**: Usually **shorter**, ideally **1–2 pages**, focusing on brevity and relevance.

### 2. **Purpose and Use**
- **CV**: Commonly used in **academic, scientific, medical, or research fields**. It's standard for job applications in **Europe, Asia, Africa, and Australia**. Also used for **grant applications, fellowships, or academic positions**.
- **Resume**: Preferred in **industry, business, and corporate sectors**, especially in the **United States and Canada**. Used for most non-academic job applications.

### 3. **Content**
- **CV**:
  - Includes **education** in detail (degrees, institutions, dates, thesis topics).
  - Lists **research experience**, **publications**, **presentations**, **grants**, **awards**, **teaching

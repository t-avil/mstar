The terms **CV** (Curriculum Vitae) and **resume** are often used interchangeably, but they have distinct differences in length, content, and usage depending on the region and context. Here's a breakdown:

### 1. **Length**
- **CV**: Typically **longer**, often **2+ pages** (can be longer depending on experience).
- **Resume**: Usually **shorter**, ideally **1 page** (2 pages max for experienced professionals).

### 2. **Purpose & Usage**
- **CV**: 
  - Commonly used in **academic, research, medical, or international contexts** (especially in Europe, Asia, Africa, and Australia).
  - Used when applying for **academic positions, fellowships, grants, or research roles**.
  - Emphasizes **education, publications, research, teaching experience, and academic achievements**.
- **Resume**:
  - Standard in **job applications**, especially in the **U.S. and Canada**.
  - Tailored to a **specific job**, highlighting **relevant skills, work experience, and accomplishments**.
  - Focuses on **what you can do for the employer**.

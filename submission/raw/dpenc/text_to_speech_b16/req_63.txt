**Opportunity cost** is an economic concept that refers to the value of the **next best alternative** that you give up when making a decision. In other words, it's what you **lose out on** when you choose one option over another.

### Key Points:
- **It's not just about money** – opportunity cost includes time, effort, and other resources.
- It's **not the total cost** of all alternatives, but only the **best one you didn't choose**.
- It helps individuals, businesses, and governments make **more informed decisions** by considering trade-offs.

### Example:
Imagine you have $100 and must choose between:
1. Buying a new laptop for $100, or  
2. Going on a vacation that costs $100.

If you choose the laptop, the **opportunity cost** is the **value of the vacation** you gave up.  
If you choose the vacation, the **opportunity cost** is the **value of the laptop** you didn't buy.

Even if you don't pay money for the vacation (e.g., it's free), the opportunity cost is still the **value of the time and experience** you could have had with the laptop.

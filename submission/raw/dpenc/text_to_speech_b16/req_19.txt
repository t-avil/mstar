Dehydration occurs when your body loses more fluids than it takes in, leading to an insufficient amount of water to carry out normal functions. Symptoms of dehydration can vary depending on severity but generally include:

**Mild to Moderate Dehydration:**
- Dry mouth and lips
- Increased thirst
- Decreased urine output or dark yellow urine
- Dry skin
- Fatigue or weakness
- Headache
- Dizziness or lightheadedness
- Muscle cramps
- Constipation

**Severe Dehydration:**
- Very dry skin and mucous membranes
- Sunken eyes
- Rapid heartbeat and breathing
- Low blood pressure
- Low urine output or no urine for several hours
- Irritability or confusion
- Fainting or lethargy
- In infants: sunken soft spot (fontanelle), no tears when crying, dry mouth

**Infants and Young Children:**
- No wet diapers for 3 or more hours
- Dry mouth and tongue
- Sunken eyes or cheeks
- No tears when crying
- High fever
- Lethargy or irritability

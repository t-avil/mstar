Based on the visual characteristics of the food in the image, this dish is **Xiaolongbao** (also known as **soup dumplings**).

Here are the key features that identify it:

*   **Steamed Dumplings:** They are small, round dumplings with a thin, translucent wrapper, which is characteristic of dumplings steamed in a bamboo basket (a *zheng*).
*   **Pleated Top:** The dumplings have a distinct pleated top, which is a signature of the *xiaolongbao* style.
*   **Soup Filling:** The name "soup dumpling" comes from the fact that they are filled with a savory meat filling (usually pork) and a hot, flavorful broth. When steamed, the gelatin from the pork skin or bones melts into a liquid, creating the soup inside. The slight sheen on the dumplings suggests they are filled with this liquid.

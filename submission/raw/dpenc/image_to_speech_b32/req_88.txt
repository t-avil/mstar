Based on the ingredients and presentation, this dish is a **Caprese salad**.

It is a classic Italian salad, typically made with fresh ingredients like:

*   **Tomatoes:** Usually ripe, red tomatoes, often sliced.
*   **Mozzarella cheese:** A fresh, white cheese, often in the form of mozzarella balls (bocconcini) or slices.
*   **Basil:** Fresh green leaves, usually torn or chopped.
*   **Olive oil and balsamic glaze:** Drizzled on top for flavor and shine.
*   **Salt and pepper:** For seasoning.

Certainly! The concept of **supply and demand** is a fundamental principle in economics that explains how the price and quantity of goods and services are determined in a market.

### 1. **Demand**
- **Definition**: Demand refers to the quantity of a good or service that consumers are willing and able to purchase at various prices during a given period.
- **Law of Demand**: There is an inverse relationship between price and quantity demanded. As the price of a good increases, the quantity demanded decreases (and vice versa), assuming all other factors remain constant.
- **Example**: If the price of apples drops, more people will likely buy them. If the price rises, fewer people will buy them.

### 2. **Supply**
- **Definition**: Supply refers to the quantity of a good or service that producers are willing and able to offer for sale at various prices.
- **Law of Supply**: There is a direct relationship between price and quantity supplied. As the price of a good increases, producers are more willing to supply more of it (and vice versa).
- **Example**: If the price of smartphones increases, manufacturers may produce and sell more units to take advantage of higher profits.

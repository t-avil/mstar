Certainly! Here's a clear explanation of the difference between **machine learning** and **deep learning**:

### 1. **Definition**
- **Machine Learning (ML):** A broad field of artificial intelligence where computers learn from data and improve over time without being explicitly programmed. It uses algorithms to identify patterns and make decisions based on input data.
- **Deep Learning (DL):** A subset of machine learning that uses **artificial neural networks** with multiple layers (hence "deep") to model complex patterns in data. It's inspired by the human brain's structure and function.

---

### 2. **How They Work**
- **Machine Learning:**
  - Requires **feature engineering**: Humans must manually select and extract relevant features from raw data.
  - Uses algorithms like:
    - Linear/Logistic Regression
    - Decision Trees
    - Support Vector Machines (SVM)
    - Random Forests
  - Best suited for structured data (e.g., spreadsheets, databases).

- **Deep Learning:**
  - Automatically learns features from raw data (e.g., pixels in images, sound waves, text).
  - Uses **neural networks** with many layers (e.g., Convolutional Neural Networks for images, Recurrent Neural

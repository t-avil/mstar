The capital of France is **Paris**.

Paris is one of the most iconic and influential cities in the world, renowned for its rich history, art, fashion, cuisine, and romantic ambiance. Located on the River Seine in northern France, Paris is not only the political and economic heart of the country but also a global cultural hub.

**Key Features of Paris:**

1. **Historical Significance**: Paris has been a center of power and culture for centuries. It played a crucial role in the French Revolution and has been home to numerous historical landmarks, including the Louvre Palace, Notre-Dame Cathedral, and the Eiffel Tower.

2. **Landmarks and Monuments**:
   - **Eiffel Tower**: Built for the 1889 World's Fair, it has become the most recognizable symbol of Paris and France.
   - **Notre-Dame Cathedral**: A masterpiece of French Gothic architecture, located on the Île de la Cité.
   - **Louvre Museum**: One of the world's largest and most visited art museums, housing masterpieces like the Mona Lisa and the Venus de Milo.
   - **Sacré-Cœur Basilica**: Perched atop Montmartre, offering panoramic views of the city.

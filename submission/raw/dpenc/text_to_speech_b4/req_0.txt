The capital of France is **Paris**.

Paris is one of the most iconic and influential cities in the world, renowned for its rich history, art, culture, fashion, and architecture. Located on the River Seine in northern France, Paris is not only the political and economic heart of the country but also a global center for art, fashion, gastronomy, and design.

**Key Features of Paris:**

1. **Historical Significance**: Paris has been a major European city for over 2,000 years. It played a central role in the French Revolution and has been a hub of intellectual and artistic movements, including the Enlightenment, Romanticism, and the Impressionist art movement.

2. **Landmarks and Monuments**:
   - **Eiffel Tower**: Perhaps the most recognizable symbol of Paris, this iron lattice tower was built for the 1889 World's Fair and remains a global icon.
   - **Notre-Dame Cathedral**: A masterpiece of French Gothic architecture, located on the Île de la Cité. Though damaged by a fire in 2019, restoration efforts are ongoing.

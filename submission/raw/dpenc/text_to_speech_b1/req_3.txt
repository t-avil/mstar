The French Revolution (1789–1799) was a period of radical social and political upheaval in France that overthrew the monarchy, ended the ancien régime, and led to the rise of democratic ideals. Sparked by widespread discontent with inequality, financial crisis, and Enlightenment ideas, it involved key events like the Storming of the Bastille, the Reign of Terror, and the rise and fall of Napoleon Bonaparte. Ultimately, it transformed France into a republic and laid the foundation for modern democratic governance.<|im_end|>

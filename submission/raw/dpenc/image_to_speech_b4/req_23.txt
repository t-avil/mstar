Here are the key features that identify it:

*   **Steamed Dumplings:** They are small, round dumplings that have been cooked by steaming, as indicated by the condensation on the dumpling skins and the bamboo steamer basket they are served in.
*   **Pleated Top:** The dumplings have a distinctive pleated top, which is pinched to seal the filling inside.
*   **Filling:** This is the most defining characteristic. Xiaolongbao are famous for containing a hot, savory broth (often made from pork gelatin) and minced meat (typically pork) inside the thin, delicate wrapper. When you bite into them, the broth bursts out.

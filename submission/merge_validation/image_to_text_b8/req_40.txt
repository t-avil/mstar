Based on the visual evidence in the image, the dish shown is **Thai Fried Rice**, also commonly known as **Khao Pad**.

Here are the key characteristics that lead to this identification:

*   **Mounded Shape:** The rice is cooked and then molded into a dome shape, which is a very common presentation for this dish.
*   **Color:** The rice has a distinct yellowish hue, which is characteristic of Thai fried rice. This color typically comes from turmeric and/or egg, which are mixed into the rice during cooking.
*   **Garnishes:** The dish is served with classic garnishes that are almost always included:
    *   **Lime Wedge:** A wedge of lime is placed on the plate, intended to be squeezed over the rice to add a fresh, acidic flavor.
    *   **Cucumber Slices:** Sliced cucumbers are served on the side, providing a cool and refreshing contrast to the savory rice.
    *   **Tomato Wedge:** A slice of tomato adds a touch of sweetness and color.
    *   **Spring Onions:** A few stalks of green onions (scallions) are placed on top, adding a mild onion flavor and a pop of green color.

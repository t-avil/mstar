Based on the visual characteristics of the food in the image, this dish is **Xiaolongbao** (also known as **soup dumplings**).

Here are the key features that identify it:

*   **Pleated, Dome-Shaped Wrapper:** The dumplings have a distinctive pleated top, which is pinched to seal the filling inside. This is a hallmark of Xiaolongbao.
*   **Steamed Preparation:** They are served in a bamboo steamer basket, which is the traditional and most common method for cooking them.
*   **Delicate Dough:** The wrapper appears thin and translucent, which is necessary to contain the hot soup filling without breaking during steaming.

Xiaolongbao are a famous type of **Shanghai cuisine**. The defining feature of this dish is the savory, hot soup (often made from pork stock and gelatin) that is enclosed within the dumpling.

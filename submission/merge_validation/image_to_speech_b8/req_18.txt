Based on the ingredients and presentation, this dish is a **Seafood Paella**.

Here are the key details that lead to this identification:

*   **The Pan:** The dish is served in a wide, shallow, black pan known as a *paellera*, which is specifically used for cooking paella.
*   **The Ingredients:** The pan is filled with saffron-infused rice, which gives it the characteristic golden-orange color. Mixed in with the rice are various types of seafood, including:
    *   **Mussels:** The dark, bivalve shells are clearly visible.

    *   **Saffron Broth:** The liquid base is a rich, yellow-orange broth, typical of paella, which gets its color from saffron threads.
*   **The Garnish:** The dish is garnished with lemon wedges, which are traditionally served with paella to be squeezed over the top before eating.
